Privacy Policy

Updated November 1, 2026

In brief

The contact form is a local preview and does not send messages. Cloudflare hosts this website and provides browser performance measurements, as described below. This website does not provide visitor accounts or use advertising systems.

Data that may be processed

The contact form includes fields for your name, email address, optional phone number, purpose and message. Values entered into this preview stay in your browser and are not sent to or stored by this website. The hosting provider may automatically process technical request logs, including an IP address, browser type, and the date and time of a request, to maintain security and reliable website operation.

Performance measurements

This website loads Cloudflare's real user monitoring (RUM) script to measure page loading and interaction performance. It sends performance measurements and page and referrer information to Cloudflare. Cloudflare states that this script does not use cookies or browser storage. These measurements are separate from hosting request logs and do not deliver messages from the contact form. See Cloudflare's RUM documentation for details.

Fonts

The Dancing Script typeface is hosted on this website. Your browser downloads it from the same domain as the page, without connecting to an external font provider.

Local interactions

Expanding the Latest section, scrolling the page, and other visual interactions are handled directly in your browser. Their state is not sent to the website owner and is reset when the page is reloaded.

External platforms

Social and music platform icons, as well as the Explore buttons, do not currently open external links or load embedded content from those services. If these features are added, the privacy policies of the respective external websites will apply.

Contact

Opening the contact panel or pressing Send does not currently transmit form values or deliver a message. Closing the panel or reloading the page clears the values from this website's form. Before message delivery is enabled, this policy will be updated with information about how messages are processed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the website’s features evolve. The date of the latest update will always be shown on this page.

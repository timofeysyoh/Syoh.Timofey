Privacy Policy

Updated September 15, 2026

In brief

At present, this website does not contain forms, use analytics or advertising systems, or set its own cookies. Interactive elements work without creating a visitor profile.

Data that may be processed

The website does not request your name, email address, phone number, or other personal data. However, the hosting provider may automatically process technical request logs, including an IP address, browser type, and the date and time of a request, to maintain security and reliable website operation.

Google Fonts

To display the Dancing Script typeface, the website loads files through Google Fonts. During this process, your browser connects directly to Google domains, so the service receives technical request data in accordance with the Google Fonts privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Local interactions

Expanding the Latest section, scrolling the page, and other visual interactions are handled directly in your browser. Their state is not sent to the website owner and is reset when the page is reloaded.

External platforms

Social and music platform icons, as well as the Explore buttons, do not currently open external links or load embedded content from those services. If these features are added, the privacy policies of the respective external websites will apply.

Contact

The Contact button does not currently transmit any data. Before a form or another contact method is enabled, this policy will be updated with information about how messages are processed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the website’s features evolve. The date of the latest update will always be shown on this page.

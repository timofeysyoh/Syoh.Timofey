Privacy Policy

Updated November 7, 2026

Website operator and scope

This website is operated by Syoh Timofey (Сьох Тимофій). For privacy questions or requests about your personal information, email timofeysyoh@gmail.com. This policy describes the website as it currently works. The website does not currently collect payments, record audio or video, or accept uploads of podcast recordings.

In brief

When message delivery is enabled, submitting the contact or podcast application form sends your information to the organiser by email through Cloudflare and Resend. If delivery is unavailable, the form reports an error instead of confirming a submission. Cloudflare hosts this website and provides browser performance measurements, as described below. This website does not provide visitor accounts or use advertising systems.

Data that may be processed

The Contact form includes your name, email, purpose of contact, optional phone number, message and confirmation checkbox. The podcast form includes your name, email, type of request, social media username, selected network, message and a checkbox confirming accuracy and that you have read the Podcast Terms. Information you enter stays in the form until you submit it. Submission sends the relevant fields and checkbox confirmation for handling your enquiry or reviewing your application. This website does not save form values in cookies or browser storage. The hosting provider may automatically process technical request logs, including an IP address, browser type, and the date and time of a request, to maintain security and reliable website operation.

Form delivery and spam protection

A Cloudflare Pages Function validates submitted fields and uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated abuse. The Turnstile script is requested when you attempt to send a form, rather than when you first open the page or form. Cloudflare processes verification signals such as IP address, browser information and the site involved, as explained in its Turnstile Privacy Addendum. Verification does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

After validation, Resend processes the email for delivery to the organiser's Gmail mailbox at timofeysyoh@gmail.com. Your email address is used as the reply address so the organiser can respond directly. The website does not send an automatic confirmation email to you. A success message means the email service accepted the message for delivery; it does not guarantee inbox placement or a reply. An error can occur after a request has reached a service, so a missing success message is not a guarantee that no data was processed.

These providers may process message content and technical records, including outside your country, under their service arrangements. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy, Resend's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy. The form handler does not write submitted message contents to application logs, but providers may keep their own delivery, security and service records.

Performance measurements

This website loads Cloudflare's real user monitoring (RUM) script to measure page loading and interaction performance. It sends performance measurements and page and referrer information to Cloudflare. Cloudflare states that this script does not use cookies or browser storage. These measurements are separate from hosting request logs and do not deliver messages or podcast applications. See Cloudflare's RUM documentation for details.

Fonts

The Dancing Script typeface is hosted on this website. Your browser downloads it from the same domain as the page, without connecting to an external font provider.

Local interactions

Expanding the Latest section, scrolling the page, and other visual interactions are handled directly in your browser. Their state is not sent to the website owner and is reset when the page is reloaded.

External platforms

Social media and podcast platform icons are image files served by this website. Displaying them does not contact those platforms or load an embedded player. These icons are not currently external platform links. The Podcast card and Podcast Terms link open pages on this website. External documentation links in this policy open other websites, whose own privacy policies apply.

Contact and podcast applications

We use submitted information to respond to contact enquiries, consider guest suggestions and podcast applications, and discuss a requested service or collaboration. The website does not maintain a separate application database. Accepted messages, including the checkbox acknowledgement, can be retained in the organiser's mailbox and in delivery-provider records. Closing the form removes its fields from the page; it does not withdraw an email already submitted. Your browser's own autofill or synchronisation features may retain information independently. The podcast checkbox is not permission to record or publish a guest; recording permissions must be agreed separately as described in the Podcast Terms. An application is not a confirmed booking or payment agreement.

The organiser retains correspondence while needed to handle the enquiry or application, follow up an agreed arrangement and meet applicable legal obligations. Afterwards it is kept only where a legal retention obligation or necessary legal-claim purpose requires it; otherwise it is deleted. Provider-controlled records are subject to the providers' retention arrangements. Please do not include sensitive information, another person's private details or material that is unnecessary for the request.

Privacy requests

Contact timofeysyoh@gmail.com about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning information actually processed by the website operator. Rights to object, restrict processing, withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority apply where provided by the relevant law. We will handle requests under that law. If you email us, your address and the contents of your request are sent to the organiser's Gmail mailbox to handle your enquiry, whether you write directly or use an enabled form. Correspondence is used to respond and meet applicable legal obligations. It is retained while needed to resolve the request, and afterwards only where a legal retention obligation or a necessary legal-claim purpose requires it; otherwise it is deleted. Information about how Google provides Gmail is available in Google's Privacy Policy. Please do not email sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the website’s features evolve. The date of the latest update will always be shown on this page.

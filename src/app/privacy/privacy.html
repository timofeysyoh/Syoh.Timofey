Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

In brief

The contact and podcast application forms are local previews and do not send messages or applications. Cloudflare hosts this website and provides browser performance measurements, as described below. This website does not provide visitor accounts or use advertising systems.

Data that may be processed

The forms include fields for contact details and a message or podcast proposal. The contact panel also includes an optional phone number. Values entered into these previews stay in your browser and are not sent to or stored by this website. The hosting provider may automatically process technical request logs, including an IP address, browser type, and the date and time of a request, to maintain security and reliable website operation.

Performance measurements

This website loads Cloudflare's real user monitoring (RUM) script to measure page loading and interaction performance. It sends performance measurements and page and referrer information to Cloudflare. Cloudflare states that this script does not use cookies or browser storage. These measurements are separate from hosting request logs and do not deliver messages or podcast applications. See Cloudflare's RUM documentation for details.

Fonts

The Dancing Script typeface is hosted on this website. Your browser downloads it from the same domain as the page, without connecting to an external font provider.

Local interactions

Expanding the Latest section, scrolling the page, and other visual interactions are handled directly in your browser. Their state is not sent to the website owner and is reset when the page is reloaded.

External platforms

The Podcast card opens another page on this website. This website does not load embedded audio or video players from external platforms. Platform links, when available, open the respective external websites, whose own privacy policies apply.

Contact and podcast applications

The form previews do not currently transmit form values or deliver messages or applications. Closing either form panel clears its values. Leaving the podcast page or reloading the page also clears form values. Before delivery is enabled, this policy will be updated with information about how messages and applications are processed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the website’s features evolve. The date of the latest update will always be shown on this page.
